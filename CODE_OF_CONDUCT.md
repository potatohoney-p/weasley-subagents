# Code of Conduct

Weasley Open Source is committed to a respectful, harassment-free community.

Be considerate, assume good intent, discuss technical disagreements with evidence, respect privacy, and avoid discriminatory, threatening, or abusive behavior. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to `potatohoney.p@gmail.com`. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project elsewhere.
